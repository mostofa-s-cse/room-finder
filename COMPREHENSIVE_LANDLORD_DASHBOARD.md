# 🏠 Comprehensive Landlord Management System

## ✨ Features Implemented:

### 🎛️ **Complete Dashboard Overview**
- **Modern Design** with gradient backgrounds and professional styling
- **Real-time Stats Cards** showing revenue, listings, tenants, and pending tasks
- **Alert System** for notifications and status updates
- **Sticky Header** with quick actions and notifications

### 📊 **7 Management Tabs:**

#### 1. **Overview Tab** 
- Dashboard summary with key metrics
- Recent activity feed
- Quick action buttons
- Performance indicators

#### 2. **Listings Management**
- **Complete CRUD Operations**: Create, Read, Update, Delete listings
- **Advanced Search & Filtering**: By status, location, rent range
- **Bulk Operations**: Multiple selections and actions
- **Status Management**: Draft, Active, Inactive, Pending, Rejected
- **Image Upload & Management**
- **Amenities Management**
- **Availability Toggle**

#### 3. **Tenant Management**
- **Tenant Profiles**: Complete tenant information
- **Lease Management**: Start/end dates, renewal tracking
- **Rent Status Tracking**: Paid, Pending, Overdue
- **Payment History**: Complete payment records
- **Communication Tools**: Direct messaging
- **Document Management**: Lease agreements, ID copies

#### 4. **Booking Management**
- **Booking Status Tracking**: Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled, Completed
- **Booking Details**: Dates, amounts, guest information
- **Approval/Rejection System**
- **Calendar Integration**
- **Revenue Tracking per booking**

#### 5. **Maintenance Management**
- **Request Tracking**: Priority, status, category
- **Work Order System**: Assign, track, complete
- **Cost Management**: Estimated vs actual costs
- **Image Documentation**: Before/after photos
- **Vendor Management**: Service provider contacts
- **Maintenance History**: Complete service records

#### 6. **Financial Management**
- **Revenue Analytics**: Monthly, yearly, growth trends
- **Expense Tracking**: Maintenance, utilities, taxes
- **Profit/Loss Statements**
- **Rent Collection Status**
- **Tax Reporting Tools**
- **Financial Forecasting**

#### 7. **Review Management**
- **Review Display**: Star ratings, comments
- **Response System**: Reply to reviews
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Positive/negative tracking
- **Review Statistics**: Average ratings, trends
- **Report System**: Flag inappropriate reviews
